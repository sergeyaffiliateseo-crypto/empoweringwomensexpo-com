What Is an AI Girlfriend App and Who Is It For?

An AI girlfriend app is software that pairs a large language model with a character layer: a name, a backstory, a look and a communication style. You chat by text, and many apps add voice messages, selfies, generated images or even video. Unlike a general assistant such as ChatGPT, these apps are tuned for ongoing relationship-style conversation rather than answering questions.

People use them for very different reasons. Some want light entertainment and banter after work. Others use a companion for practicing conversation, easing loneliness or exploring creative storylines. A smaller group is mainly interested in adult content. None of these motivations is unusual, and a good review should respect all of them while staying honest about the trade-offs.

How We Test and Score Every AI Girlfriend
