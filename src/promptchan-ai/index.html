What Is PromptChan AI

Short answer: PromptChan AI is a dual-purpose service: a text-to-image and image-to-video generator for adult-oriented content, and an AI companion chat with voice messages and voice calls.

Most reviews cover only one half. Image-focused pages treat it as an art tool, while girlfriend-app pages barely mention the generator. For readers of this site, the duality matters. If you want a companion first, you will find simpler experiences at Candy AI or DreamGF. If you want to create your own imagery, PromptChan gives you more raw control than most chat-first apps.

Companions come in realistic and anime styles, and you can use pre-built characters or custom ones, although the catalog size is not published. The site claims 11,184,140 users, which is a vendor claim we cannot verify. Content is adult-oriented, and a Content Moderation Policy page is linked from the homepage.

The operator is not named on the site [VERIFY], and the launch date is not stated; the iOS app identifier suggests the product dates from around 2023 [VERIFY]. We return to what that means for trust in the safety section. A related generator-first service is Seduced AI, which makes a useful point of comparison.

Image Generation: Styles, Quality Tiers and Tweak

Short answer: PromptChan generates images from text prompts in realistic ("hyperreal") and anime styles, with quality tiers that cost 1 to 3 gems and tools such as seed values, Tweak and Clone for control.

The core workflow is simple: write a prompt, choose a style and a quality tier, then generate. Reported gem costs per image (as of November 2026) [VERIFY]:

Quality tier Reported gem cost
Standard 1 gem
High 1 gem
Extreme 2 gems
Max / 4K 3 gems

The control tools are what set the platform apart:

  • Seed value: reuse a seed to reproduce or vary a look, which helps with consistency across images.
  • Tweak: change specific details such as outfit or lighting without restarting from scratch.
  • Clone: copy a prompt from the community gallery and adapt it, which shortens the learning curve for newcomers.
  • Explore gallery: browse what others have made for inspiration.

Reviewers praise the anime and fantasy output in particular, while hyperreal results vary more with prompting skill. Character consistency, meaning the same face across many images, is helped by seeds but is not guaranteed. If consistency is crucial, OurDream AI and Secrets AI are worth testing too.

Video Generation: Pro Plan, Clip Length and Gem Cost

Short answer: PromptChan offers image-to-video generation (Video v4 with an Extend option), reported to require the Pro plan and about 15 gems per clip of roughly 5 to 10 seconds, though sources disagree [VERIFY].

Whether the platform does video at all is a point of confusion. A competitor's comparison page (ZenCreator) claims PromptChan has no video, but the vendor's own site and several reviewers describe it. We side with the vendor's site on existence, and treat the details with caution.

What the sources say:

  • Video is image-to-video: you start from a generated or uploaded image.
  • It requires the Pro plan and gems.
  • A clip costs about 15 gems, and clips run about 5 to 10 seconds.
  • Extend lets you lengthen a clip.

With a Pro allowance of around 1,500 gems, 15 gems per clip works out to a theoretical 100 clips a month if you did nothing else, but images and chat draw from the same pool. Plan for far fewer. If long clips are your main goal, Seduced AI advertises up to 18-second clips, so check our Seduced AI review before deciding. Video also adds the main reason to be careful with personal images: only upload pictures you have the right to use.

AI Companions and Chat: Characters, Voice Messages and Calls

Short answer: the companion side offers pre-built and custom characters in realistic and anime styles, with voice messages and voice calls, but reviewers describe the chat as less deep than dedicated companion apps.

This is the part that should interest readers of an AI girlfriend site. You can pick a character, chat in text and use voice messages or calls. That already goes beyond a pure generator. However, memory, personality depth and long-term relationship features are not highlighted, and no review we found tests them thoroughly.

Compared with specialists, the gap is clear. Nomi AI is better for continuity, and Candy AI offers a more polished companion experience. If you want an image generator that also lets you chat, PromptChan fits well. If you want a girlfriend who happens to send pictures, choose a chat-first product. Free-tier numbers differ between sources. One review says about 10 gems per day, while another describes "5 to 10 images per day." These can both be true, since a standard image costs 1 gem and higher tiers cost more.

The free tier is restrictive by design. You can test the style and the interface, but you cannot do serious work or test video. If you like the output, move to a paid plan; if not, you have spent nothing.

How Gems Work and Real Monthly Cost Scenarios

Short answer: gems are PromptChan's single currency for images, video and some chat actions, included monthly with each plan, buyable in packs of 50 for about $0.99 and reported to roll over between months [VERIFY].

The rollover claim is an advantage if true: unused gems do not vanish at month end. Since the price per gem is roughly 2 cents in top-up packs, you can estimate costs:

  • Casual image user on Plus: 300 gems covers roughly 300 standard images, so about $10 per month is enough.
  • Quality-focused user on Premium: 800 gems at 3 gems per 4K image gives about 266 4K images, at roughly $15 to $19 per month.
  • Video user on Pro: 1,500 gems at 15 gems per clip allows at most 100 clips, and realistically 40 to 60 once you also make images, at about $22 to $27 per month.
  • Free user: about 10 gems per day, so around 300 per month if you log in daily.

These are our own estimates based on reported gem costs, and per-action prices have changed over time according to reviewers [VERIFY]. The main frustration across reviews is opacity: costs shift, and some features have been removed without notice. Always check the gem cost displayed before pressing generate, and keep a running tally in your first month.

Is PromptChan AI Safe and Legit?

Short answer: PromptChan appears to be a working, widely used service with a moderation policy and a refund policy page, but the operator is not disclosed, support is reported as slow and features have changed without notice, so treat it with measured caution.

Positive signs: the site is live and heavily visited, it links a Content Moderation Policy and a Refund Policy, and reviewers confirm that the product works as described. A rating of about 7.2 out of 10 on techraisal reflects solid output quality.

Concerns:

  • The operator and legal entity are not stated on the site [VERIFY].
  • Payment methods are not described on the homepage [VERIFY].
  • A reviewer reports that features were removed without notice.
  • Support is described as slow.
  • Privacy defaults for generated images are not clearly explained.

Practical steps: read the refund policy before paying, use a card with a spending limit, avoid uploading real people's photos and assume that prompts may be stored. Generating images of real, identifiable people without consent is harmful and often illegal, so keep to fictional characters. Our safety and privacy guide lists further steps.
